## Why Tiger carries them

Owner's words, normalized for this brief:

- The replacement net is a standard net for any PingPong table, not only Tiger tables.
- Older Expo and Portland tables used removable metal uprights. Those pieces were easy to misplace in homes and busy shared spaces, so Tiger moved to the current net system.
- The earlier hardware is no longer available, and the earlier and current systems do not interchange piece by piece. If anything from the older hardware is missing, the complete current-system upgrade is required.
- The supplier and reordering history is internal context, not customer-facing copy. Do not imply that the change was intentional obsolescence.

## Customer and moment

### Standard Replacement Net

Primary customer: Someone with a standard PingPong table whose posts and mounting hardware are complete.
Primary setting: Tiger or non-Tiger standard PingPong tables.
Problem or frustration: The fabric net needs replacing, but the rest of the net setup is still usable.
Why the next closest Tiger product is different: The Expo & Portland Net Upgrade System replaces the complete table-specific system. The separate Net & Post Set is for a suitable tabletop or another table and is not a Tiger-table replacement system.

### Expo & Portland Net Upgrade System

Primary customer: An Expo or Portland owner who needs the complete current net setup, especially when an older removable-upright component is missing.
Primary setting: Every Tiger PingPong Expo and Portland table, indoor or outdoor.
Problem or frustration: Earlier removable pieces were easy to misplace, and current components cannot replace earlier components one at a time.
Why the next closest Tiger product is different: The Standard Replacement Net replaces only the net. Whistler uses a separate system. Plaza is not compatible.
